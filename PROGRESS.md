## Personal ski log and mountain coverage (November 9, 2026, in progress)

- **Recorder** (`client/src/log/skiLog.ts`): every frame of real play time feeds session, today and all-time totals: distance skied, vertical descended, descents, time skiing (moving on skis), time on lifts, time on the mountain, average moving speed and top valid speed, plus ski days. Durations are real seconds, independent of the resort clock and lift fast-forward. A descent starts when you move off on skis and ends when you board a lift, teleport (detected as a position jump), step out of your skis, fly the drone, or stand still for four minutes. Each keeps its date, distance, vertical, start and finish elevation, moving and elapsed time, average and top speed, the trails it covered and a decimated line (x, z, elevation, speed every 5 m or 3 s; lines kept for the 60 most recent of up to 400 descents).
- **Coverage**: 65 named, skiable trails (green to double black and the park; one entry per name, keyed by the lowest OSM way id so a rename keeps history). Each trail is cut into 10 m bins. While skiing, the bin level with you on the trail under you counts across its width (45 m); other trails count only where you actually cross or join them (4 m). A lap needs 75% of the trail's bins in one continuous descent, reaching its upper and lower fifth; partial coverage is the share ever skied (at least 50 m). Repeat laps add to the trail's lap count and each is its own history entry.
- **Panel** (`client/src/ui/skiLogPanel.ts`, pause menu **Ski log and mountain progress**): Overview (three stat cards, whole-mountain progress bar with completed and partly skied trails and counts by difficulty, recent descents including the one in progress), Run history (every descent; click for its line on the map coloured by speed, start and finish, stats and an elevation and speed profile along the distance), and Mountain map (completed, partly skied and not yet skied trails viewed from the base like the resort map, filters by difficulty, and a list with laps, share skied and the last lap date). Saved in this browser's localStorage under `sunlight.skilog.v1`; Clear ski log resets it.
- Verified in normal play with the real keyboard input path (`tests/scratch/skilog_play.mjs`): a full Midway descent completed the trail, part of Sun King showed 46% partly skied, a Segundo ride added lift time, everything survived a reload, and a second Midway lap made two laps and three history entries with no stray credit on neighbouring trails. Fixed during review: crossings and junctions credited neighbouring trails, and the line colours were relative to each descent's own top speed (now 0 to 34+ mph). Screens are in `screenshots/review-skilog/`. New e2e `skiLog.spec.ts` passes in Chromium and WebKit; `lesson.spec.ts` now also runs in WebKit.
